# Davi e Golias — story bible

**Logline:** a young shepherd, overlooked by everyone, faces a giant everyone fears, because he looks at the size of God, not of the giant. Tone: epic + devotional; ends speaking to "você" (the viewer).

## Motifs
Eye vs heart ("o homem vê a aparência, Deus vê o coração"); five stones; god rays from above when God acts; freeze-frame on the thrown stone; the giant shadow for the viewer's "giants".
